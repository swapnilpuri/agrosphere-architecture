# 0004-geospatial-provenance: Canonical IDs and truthful digital twins

Status: Accepted for Milestone 0 foundation
Date: 2026-10-23

## Context
A new rubber-plantation POC needs a production-oriented foundation while keeping the first execution strictly limited to Milestone 0.

## Decision
UUID estate/block identity comes from estate. Spatial owns RFC 7946 WGS84 geometry; both clients consume authoritative APIs. Data are labeled REAL, SIMULATED, ESTIMATED or PREDICTED. Procedural trees are illustrative.

## Consequences
Coordinate/vertical-datum transformations and uncertainty must be explicit; no claims of survey accuracy without evidence.
