## Questions and choices to forward

All day counts below mean elapsed 24-hour periods measured from server UTC timestamps. A deadline is inclusive: information expires at that instant. “Inactive” means the alert actually transitions to resolved/voided/below-threshold. Incidental edits while inactive do not reset the clock. Reactivation cancels an unexpired lifecycle deadline; the next inactive transition starts a new one. Nothing restores already expired/deleted data.
