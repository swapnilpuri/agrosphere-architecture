# Product vision
AgroSphere is a cloud-native farm management and plantation intelligence platform. The first proof of concept focuses exclusively on rubber plantations. Managers will remotely explore a geospatially accurate 3D estate, inspect blocks, understand tapping/latex production, monitor weather and soil, and review evidence-backed AI recommendations.

The differentiators are an authoritative geospatial digital twin, AI-assisted operational intelligence and a shared digital estate across web/CesiumJS and Unreal clients. Later crops (tea, coffee, palm oil, cocoa, avocado) are roadmap items, not POC functionality. AR/VR/MR follow an explicit feasibility milestone.

Success is a reproducible local demonstration with traceable block IDs and provenance labels. It must distinguish observed reality from simulated data, estimates and predictions. No POC recommendation is an autonomous agronomic decision.
